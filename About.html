About Chalfont Valley PTA

Bringing our school community together, creating memories and making a difference for every child.

Welcome to the Chalfont Valley PTA website! We are a friendly group of parents, carers and volunteers who work together to support our school and bring families closer together.

Through fundraising, school events and community activities, we aim to make a positive difference for all our children.

What We Do

  • Organise fun school events.
  • Raise funds for school resources.
  • Bring parents and families together.
  • Support our school community.
