Welcome to Chalfont Valley PTA

Bringing our school community together, creating memories and making a difference for every child.

Welcome to our PTA! We are a friendly group of parents, carers and volunteers who work together to support our school and bring families closer together.

From fun events and exciting activities to fundraising and community projects, there are lots of ways to get involved. Every little contribution makes a difference.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a happy, connected and thriving school community where every child has the opportunity to shine.

We believe that when parents, teachers and families work together, amazing things can happen. Every idea, helping hand and contribution matters.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring people together, raise valuable funds and create exciting opportunities that enrich every child's school experience.

Through teamwork, creativity and community spirit, we aim to make a lasting difference to our school.

What We Do

  • Organise fun events for children and families.
  • Raise funds to support our school.
  • Bring parents, carers and teachers together.
  • Create memorable experiences for our children.
  • Support projects that benefit our school community.
  • Explore our Fun Calendar

Get Involved!

You don't need lots of spare time to make a difference. Whether you can help at an event, share an idea or lend a hand occasionally, we would love to hear from you.

Together, we can make Chalfont Valley an even more wonderful place for our children.
